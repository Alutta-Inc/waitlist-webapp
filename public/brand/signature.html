Put it in Gmail

  1. Press Copy signature above.
  2. In Gmail, open Settings, then See all settings, and scroll to Signature.
  3. Create a signature, click into the box and paste.
  4. Under Signature defaults, choose the address it belongs to (for a group address, the one you send as), for new emails and for replies.
  5. Tick Insert signature before quoted text, then save at the bottom of the page.

A group address such as partnerships@alutta.com has no settings of its own. Each person who answers from it adds the address under Accounts, Send mail as, and sets this signature for that address in their own Gmail.
